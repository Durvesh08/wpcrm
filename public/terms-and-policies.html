  • Admin: Authorized to configure WhatsApp connections, train knowledge base embeddings, manage automations, and provision agent seats.
  • Agent: Authorized solely for assigned inbox chat handling, contact tagging, and operational deal movement.
Security Imperative: API Keys & Webhook Protection

Customer is solely responsible for safeguarding administrative credentials, multi-factor authentication tokens, Meta App Secrets, and ZOVAIX REST API keys. ZOVAIX disclaims all liability for data exposure or unintended automated messaging resulting from compromised Customer infrastructure.

SECTION 02

Subscription Plans, Billing, Seats & Pass-Through Fees

2.1 Subscription Tiers & Automatic Billing

ZOVAIX operates on a recurring subscription model (billed on either a monthly or annual prepaid cadence). All recurring subscriptions automatically renew on their scheduled billing date unless Customer submits a formal cancellation through the In-App Billing Center prior to the renewal timestamp.

2.2 Named Seat Licensing & Mid-Cycle Adjustments

Seats are provisioned on a named-user basis. Each unique seat license permits access for one designated human operator. Mid-cycle additions of seats or tier upgrades are charged on an immediate prorated basis for the remaining days of the current billing cycle. Reductions in seat counts take effect at the conclusion of the active paid period; no mid-term seat credits are issued.

2.3 WhatsApp Conversation Pass-Through Fees

Customer acknowledges that WhatsApp (Meta Platforms, Inc.) assesses independent telecommunications fees based on 24-hour conversation sessions across distinct categories:

Utility Order receipts, delivery updates, confirmations Direct Meta Pass-Through (Rate by Country) Strictly Non-Refundable
Authentication One-time passwords (OTP), login verification Direct Meta Pass-Through (Rate by Country) Strictly Non-Refundable
